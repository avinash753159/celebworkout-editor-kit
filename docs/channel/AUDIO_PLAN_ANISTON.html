I. The rule that governs everything below

Never slice narration per beat

16 beats do not mean 16 audio files. Per-beat AAC re-encoding puts an audible glitch at every join. The script is written and recorded as five unbroken continuous-narration blocks (CNBs); beat boundaries inside a block are handled by pacing and emphasis in the copy, not by a file cut. Hard VO stops happen only at the 8 boundary points in the timeline below.

Target narration pace 140–155 wpm throughout. At ~1,130s of continuous narration across the runtime, script length should land ~2,650–2,900 words total — trued up against the edge-tts draft, not guessed. Recording workflow: run the entire locked script through free edge-tts first, cut it into the 5 CNB files, drop them against picture to confirm sync/pacing across all 125 shot rows, revise until locked — only then re-record the final 5 files with paid TTS. One paid pass per CNB, five renders total, never re-run per-beat or per-revision.

III. Music

The house bed

Cinematic drone + subtle percussion, 85 BPM, minor key (exact key TBD by composer — do not modulate between cues). Deep kick, snare on 2 & 4, off-beat hi-hats, bass on a root progression — matches this channel's other films' bed style. Built once, reused across the film via arrangement layering, not tempo change.

IV. Mix rules

  • Music sits at -18dB relative to narration for the full duration of every VO-continuous block. Confirm by meter, not by ear.
  • Only the two true music-only stretches (0:00–0:10, 19:40–20:00) allow music above the -18dB bed — nowhere else, even if a beat feels like it wants a breather. This channel's density rule means silence is concentrated, not sprinkled.
  • 2s fade-in at the top of the film and at any full music reintroduction after a drop-out; 4s fade-out at the true end and at any point music fully stops.
  • ffmpeg amix must run with normalize=0 so short inputs (bite clips, stingers) aren't auto-attenuated relative to the long narration/music beds.
  • Both the narration track and the music bed must pass through aformat=channel_layouts=stereo before the amix stage — a mono layer under a stereo bed otherwise sums roughly +3dB hot.
  • Narration is exported and edited as 5 unbroken CNB files, never sliced at individual beat boundaries. Cuts only occur at the 6 interludes/bookends, and every cut lands on a narration file boundary, never mid-sentence.
  • Percussion (kick, snare, hats, bass) is introduced or stripped only at the 6 interlude points — never swelled mid-CNB, so it never pulls focus from continuous narration.
  • Recording sequence: full script drafted and timed against free edge-tts across all 5 CNBs first. Paid TTS records once, per CNB, only after script and timing are fully locked — never re-run mid-revision.
  • Consider light sidechain compression keyed from the narration track onto the music bed, on top of the static -18dB level, so hi-hat transients and consonant-heavy passages don't mask each other.
  • Lock relative levels and fades first; apply final integrated-loudness normalization (e.g. -14 LUFS for YouTube) as the last step on the fully mixed output, not before.
