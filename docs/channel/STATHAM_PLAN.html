§2The workflow inversion, precisely

The old pipeline wrote a script, paid for the voice, then built pictures to match. That's backwards for cost: every script change after hearing it re-burns credits. The new order builds the film first. A draft script drives a free machine voice (edge-tts) that exists only to carry timing; every clip, card, interlude, and effect is assembled against it; you watch the full draft and mark up anything — clips, wording, pacing, cards. Edits are free because nothing paid exists yet. Only when you say locked does Brian read the final script — once — and the assembly re-syncs automatically to his word timings.

Brian reads the locked script in a single generation. Whisper re-times it, beats re-cut automatically (cut windows carry slack for exactly this), cards re-sync word-timed entrances, assembly re-runs mechanically. No creative decisions left — just rendering.

Every gate from the Reynolds grind + the new audio gate

Duration ±1s, blackdetect, freeze-scan, class-aware uniqueness, frame-verified graphics and cuts, segment level-match audit (the check that would have caught both V9 audio bugs), two-pass linear loudnorm at −14 LUFS. Then: JASON_STATHAM_V1.mp4 + Drive link + OneDrive sync.
