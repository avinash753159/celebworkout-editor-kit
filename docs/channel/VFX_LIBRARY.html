I. How this library works

Three rules make this a library rather than a mood board.

Rule 1 — pin by watching

An effect is specified by downloading the section, extracting frames, and looking at them — never by describing it from memory. Every entry below marked PINNED has frames on disk under dossier/references/. The old catalogue filed the Johnny Harris reference as circles-and-arrows for weeks; two minutes of actually watching it proved that wrong.

Rule 2 — one job, one treatment

Consistency is not "use good effects." It is the same treatment for the same job, every film. §IV maps beat type → effect. Improvising here is precisely what reads as "it wasn't one brand theme, it was just cut and paste."

Rule 3 — the contract

effect(sources, frames, **params) -> Path, returning exactly frames frames at 1920×1080/24. The assembler refuses anything else. Every effect here costs nothing per shot — they composite material already owned rather than generating new material.

II. Pinned — specified from frames

text_matte pinned
Footage plays through the type
Cleo Abram, What If You Just Keep Digging? @ 1:07
dossier/references/cleo_abram_67/ · 12 frames

The letters are a mask, not type over video. Footage plays through the glyphs; everything outside them is near-black, so the letters are the only light in frame.

Scale
Enormous. Two lines of condensed caps filling the frame, deliberately overflowing the edges — "CRUST" runs off the right. It should feel too big for the frame.
Ground
Near-black, not pure black — faint dark texture remains visible outside the glyphs.
Fill
The brightest, most active footage available. Here, molten rock. Movement inside the letters is the point.
Type
Anton, all caps, tight tracking, max two lines.
Grade
Bloom where the fill footage is brightest.
Use for
Cold-open title · chapter cards · any single loud word.
source_highlight pinned · 2 variants
A published claim, shown as a physical page
Left: Johnny Harris @ 0:21 · Right: Cleo Abram @ 2:31
dossier/references/johnny_harris_0021/ · cleo_abram_151/

The channel's signature move. The last film was rejected four times for showing websites — "Again it's a website. I did not want this." — while the rules demand that a claim resting on a published source shows the actual page. This is the resolution: it shows the source without looking like a screenshot.

Scale
Macro, never a page view. 5–8 words span the full frame width. You are reading over a shoulder.
Focus
The highlighted line is the only sharp band in frame. One line out is soft, two lines out is heavily blurred. This is the single parameter that stops it reading as a screenshot.
Highlight
Translucent band, black text fully legible through it — multiply blend, never a covering fill. Paper texture shows through.
Geometry
Off-horizontal baseline and slow drift. It must read as a page that was photographed.
Variants
Harris: coral/red, harder edge, flatter page. Abram: yellow-green highlighter, softer edge, strong perspective recede.
Movement
Abram's version tracks down the page across a single move, the highlight repositioning to successive lines rather than cutting.
Use for
Any claim resting on a document, study, article or post. No raw screenshots, ever.
Brand decision

Take the Harris variant. Coral sits inside the channel's #B22B1A family; the yellow-green highlighter does not, and would be the only place in the film where a non-brand colour carries meaning. Same geometry and focus behaviour, brand red in the band.

book_plate pinned · new
An old, authoritative physical source
Cleo Abram @ 4:31 · dossier/references/cleo_abram_271/ · 14 frames
Not previously in the catalogue.

Distinct from source_highlight: no highlight, no single-line isolation. This effect's job is to establish that a source is old and real before any claim is read off it. It buys authority.

Framing
Opens on the full spread — both pages, edge to edge, no desk or background visible. Then a slow push-in onto the plate or the passage.
Ground
Aged warm paper, sepia, low saturation. Engraving and letterpress texture must survive the grade.
Grade
Heavy vignette into all four corners — considerably stronger than source_highlight. The centre is the only lit region.
Geometry
Slight angle on the spread; the gutter is visible and curved. It is a book, not a scan.
Move
Push-in only. No pan, no cut inside the move.
Build
Reuses flow_dibr push-in with a vignette and paper grade pass. Cheap.
Use for
Citing a book, a historical record, an archive document, a published study. Chain it into source_highlight — establish the object, then read the line.
headline_over pinned
Text in front, footage behind
Cleo Abram @ 10:04
dossier/references/cleo_abram_604/ · 14 frames

This is the one you described as "video in the back and the text from the site in the front." Footage keeps playing behind; the headline sits directly on it with no box.

Darkening
The whole frame is graded down, not just a band behind the text. The presenter stays fully visible and still moving — she is not a backdrop, she is underneath.
Type
Editorial serif, not the channel grotesque — it has to read as a publication's headline rather than a caption. Left-aligned, two lines, generous leading.
Accent
The first clause is accent-coloured, the remainder warm white. Because manifest/words.json carries 1,396 word-level timings, the accent advances word by word as it is spoken rather than sitting there static.
Byline
Small italic, beneath, low contrast. "By Eric Niiler." It is what makes the graphic read as a real article rather than a title card.
Position
Vertically centred, left third. Never centred horizontally.
Use for
Quoting a headline while narration continues over footage.
Brand note

Abram's accent is the same yellow-green as her highlighter. Ours becomes #B22B1A — but red on graded-down footage risks going dark-on-dark, which is the exact failure BRAND.md already documents for title mattes. Reuse fx_text's luminance check: measure inside the glyphs and lift the footage under them when it falls below threshold.

V. The fifth agent

Agent 05
THE COMPOSITOR
Look · effects · brand integrity

Own the look. Pin every treatment to a real frame, assign one to every beat, and make every film look like the same channel made it.

Owns

  • This library — every effect, its spec, its reference frames
  • The beat → treatment assignment for each film
  • Effect implementations, held to the contract
  • Brand integrity — palette, type, device consistency, sampled across frames

Does not own

What the film says, what footage exists, or where a shot goes in the timeline. The Cutter places shots; the Compositor decides how each one is treated.

Signature move

Watch, then specify. Given a reference URL and timestamp, it downloads the section, extracts frames, reads them, and writes parameters — scale, focus, colour, geometry, movement. A reference without frames is not a specification, it is a wish.

Must refuse

Any effect not in this library · a raw screenshot where source_highlight applies · a non-brand colour carrying meaning · improvising a new treatment for a job that already has one · #E3120B

Judged by

The Editor critic, on one question: does this look like the same channel that made the last one? That is the failure it exists to prevent — "it wasn't one brand theme, it was just cut and paste."
