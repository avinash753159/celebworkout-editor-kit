The Four Pillars

Script, audio, footage, effects. Each one gets the same four-part apparatus — a standard, a library, an owner, a gate. Three of the four are missing at least one piece, and one of them is missing all four.

v1 · 3 August 2026Scored honestly, not aspirationally

I. The method — identical for all four

A pillar is mastered when four things exist. Not three. This is the whole framework, and it is deliberately the same for every pillar so that progress is comparable.

The apparatus
#PieceQuestion it answersFails without it
1A standardWhat does "good" mean, in numbers?Taste arguments with no resolution
2A libraryWhat does excellent look like, pinned to real examples?Reinvented from memory every film
3An ownerWhich agent is accountable?Nobody does it; it silently disappears
4A gateHow is it checked before it costs money?Found when you watch it — a whole night
How a gate earns trust

Every gate is validated the same way: it must convict the two films you already rejected, for the reasons in the voice note. A gate that passes MRBEAST_V6 or THE_BODY_HE_STOPPED_BUILDING is broken and does not ship. That is what makes an AI-only review defensible rather than a rubber stamp.

II. What Patrick Gavia actually does

The reference channel. ~300k subscribers, 21M+ views, sixteen documentaries, median 26.9 minutes. Four people. Everything below is from his own account, not inferred from the films.

His four production stages — in order
#StageWhat happensWhoTime
1Story research"Everything that happened throughout the subject's life"part-time researcher~1 wk
2Beat sheet"A scene-by-scene outline of what should happen in the documentary"him~2 hours
3Video researchGathering all the footage the beats call forfull-time researcher~1 wk
4EditThe cuthim, never delegatedthe rest

Team of four: a full-time video researcher, a full-time assistant editor, a full-time social and accounting person, and a part-time story researcher. He delegates everything except being on camera and the edit — "you have to accept that at first, that person will not do it as well as you… but with time they will catch up."

The 20:1 ratio

Two hours structuring. Forty hours finding footage. He spends roughly twenty times more effort on the hunt than on the plan. Your pipeline ran it inverted — three complete scripts and a full render with zero sourcing — and four minutes of real footage-searching then produced 30 named exercises and 560k characters of unused interview.

His four are stages. Ours are pillars. They cross.

This is the useful part: his four are a sequence of work, the four below are dimensions of quality. Every stage touches more than one pillar, which is why a stage can complete and a pillar still fail.

The beat sheet is two hours of work that touches all four pillars — the highest leverage document in the whole process, and the one your pipeline never had. Note also that video research is the only stage carrying the audio pillar besides the edit: bites are audio before they are picture, which is why pillar ② scores 0.5 while ③ scores 2.

What not to copy

He writes the beat sheet before the footage hunt because he knows the MMA archive by heart. You do not have that per celebrity — so an inventory pass substitutes for it: inventory → beat sheet → targeted hunt. And do not automate the edit first; it is the one thing he will not hand over even to a full-time assistant editor.

IV. The missing owner — agent 06

THE COMPOSER
Agent 06 · score · voice · mix

Make the film work with the picture turned off.

Owns
The music library and cue assignment · voice profile and delivery · the mix (levels, ducking, J-cuts) · the radio edit — the whole film as audio, gated before a cent is spent
Doesn't own
The words (Cutter) · which bites exist (Hunter)
Signature
Free draft first, paid narration once. Build and gate the entire film on edge-tts; ElevenLabs fires a single time, on the locked script. Already the stated rule in SETUP.md — it has never had an owner to enforce it.
Refuses
Narration that echoes an adjacent bite · a bite cut mid-thought · music selected per film instead of drawn from the library · paid narration on an ungated script
Judged by
The Viewer critic, listening with no picture: is this worth hearing?

One loader, one place

Right now keys are read ad hoc and SETUP.md documents a real trap — a stale GOOGLE_API_KEY in the environment silently overriding the project file. One loader removes the whole class of problem:

pipeline/keys.py       # single loader. file beats environment. prints source, masked.
