Note

The honest answer

20 minutes is reachable — but not on the first film for a new celebrity. Cold start lands near 50 minutes, because downloading eight sources is real work no trick removes. The 20-minute number arrives on the second pass, or once the corpus is warm.

IV. The lever that dwarfs all of them

Everything above optimises one pass through the pipeline. But the Hemsworth film has had three complete scripts and a full render, all binned — and the MrBeast film ran through audio v3–v6 and picture v3–v9 before you rejected it.

At eight hours a cycle, that is not an eight-hour project. It is a forty-hour project with nothing published at the end of it. Two films finished, zero shipped.
