I. The locked charter

Title

The Practice That Outlasted the Headlines — won a 3-angle tournament, 3 judges, 2-1. See dossier/jennifer_aniston/SOURCES.md for the two losing pitches and why.

Thesis — one sentence (narrowed post exemplar-pass, see GOAL.md §2 & §13)

While two marriages ended in public and tabloids tracked her body for three decades, the private daily practice underneath it — yoga with Mandy Ingber from at least 2006, then Pvolve since — stayed hers, even as each version of it acquired a name, a DVD, a company.

Two things fixed after a second, adversarial pass

(1) The original thesis claimed the practice "never had a headline attached to it" — three independent editor-critics caught the same hole: the film's hero artifacts are a commercial DVD and a company (Pvolve) Aniston is paid to be the face of. Thesis narrowed above. (2) 8 of 11 verbatim quotes in the shot list could not be independently re-confirmed on adversarial re-check (one was actively wrong). Converted to narrator paraphrase — real voice dropped from 9.7% to 3.2% as a direct, correct consequence. See GOAL.md §13 and the note field on affected rows in pipeline/shotlist_jennifer_aniston.py.
