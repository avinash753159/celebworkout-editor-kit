## Description

Michael Jordan was already the best player in basketball and kept going home
early. Detroit knocked Chicago out of the playoffs three years running with a
defensive plan built on one bet: that he was not strong enough to take a beating.

This is what he changed. The six a.m. sessions his teammates started turning up
to, the programme Tim Grover actually wrote, what he ate four hours before every
game, and why the era's advice about carbohydrates did not apply to him.

Featuring recorded interviews with Michael Jordan, his personal trainer Tim
Grover, and Chicago Bulls head athletic trainer Chip Schaefer — in their own
words, not paraphrased.
