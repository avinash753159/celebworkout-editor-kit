# Description + quote cards — proposed rewrite

Both had the same fault: they describe the *genre* instead of the *video*.

---

## 1. The YouTube description

### What is there now

> The celebrities we feature on this channel all have one thing in common: they
> make us question the laws of nature. Jennifer Aniston got hurt at 52, threw out
> the routine she had used for twenty years, and came back fitter than she was at
> 30. This is her full workout routine and diet plan.

Three sentences, and only one of them is about Jennifer Aniston. "Make us
question the laws of nature" is channel boilerplate. Nothing names Pvolve, Dani
Coleman, 16:8, the fasting window, or a single thing the viewer will actually
watch — which is also every term someone would have searched to find it.

### Proposed

```
At 52, on a film set, Jennifer Aniston hurt her back — and the routine she had
run for twenty years was over. Yoga with Mandy Ingber. Spinning. Running.
Boxing with Leyon Azubuike. Three to seven sessions a week, and all of it
impact.

What she replaced it with is low-impact resistance training: Pvolve, with
trainer Dani Coleman, sessions running from fifteen minutes to about fifty.
She says she is in better shape now than she was in her twenties.

This is the full breakdown — the training week day by day, and the diet
underneath it: the 16:8 fasting window, breakfast at ten, the celery juice
before the coffee, the Mexican food, and the Sunday she doesn't track
anything.

[CHAPTERS — regenerate, see note below]

The training week is assembled from methods she has described publicly. It is
not a leaked copy of her private programme, and the video says so on screen
before delivering it.

Not medical advice. If you are training around something that hurts, see
someone who can actually examine you.
```

**Chapter timestamps must be regenerated.** Moving the T1 bite out of Her Life
and into The Workout shifts every marker after 00:45. Do not copy the old ones
across.

### Why there are so many cards in the first place

Worth saying plainly, because it changes what "fix the cards" means: **Aniston's
diet is almost entirely a print record.** InStyle, Yahoo Food, Radio Times,
Entertainment Tonight write-ups. I searched YouTube hard for her talking about
food on camera and found exactly **one** genuine source. The cards are not
laziness — they are what exists.

The fix is therefore not "replace cards with clips" across the board. It is:
cut the one card that says nothing (W1), replace the one card where footage
genuinely exists (D4), and leave the three that are carrying real weight.
