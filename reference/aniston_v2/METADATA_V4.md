## Why V4 exists

V3 was built against `ytceleb/docs/BRAND.md` — a written document — and never
checked against the channel. Measuring the channel's own Jennifer Aniston video
(309,000 views) showed V3 was a different kind of object entirely.

Three specific reversals:

1. **Stock footage of other people is the format.** The reference labels
   "TEMPLE POSE, 8 REPS, 8 BREATHS" over a stock yoga model. V3 had a
   `SUBSTITUTE` quarantine that forbade exactly this. Removed.
2. **The long biography is correct.** V3's own review called it a retention
   problem. The reference spends five minutes on birth dates and cancelled
   1990 pilots.
3. **The subscribe ask should be longer, not shorter.**

Facts are still the current researched ones — the 2021 injury, Pvolve, Dani
Coleman, 16:8 — which the reference predates. Same form, current content.
