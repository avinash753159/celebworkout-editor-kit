## Honesty notes, kept deliberately

- **The week (7:30ish) is a synthesis**, assembled from methods she has
  described publicly, and the card says so on screen. Nobody outside her
  training has her actual programme.
- **The "burnt out and broke my body" quote is a print quote to InStyle**,
  read aloud by an Access Hollywood presenter. There is no footage of her
  saying it, so it stays a card and the credit says "read on air, not said on
  camera" rather than implying an interview.
- **There is no footage of Aniston boxing.** The block that names her boxing
  trainer runs on her own gym footage and a card, not on him training somebody
  else.
- **The movement names describe what she is doing on screen.** Where the
  footage could not support a named movement, the movement changed — a glute
  bridge became the kneeling row, a slant-board squat became a dumbbell squat,
  and a side-lying leg lift became a quadruped raise, because that is what the
  footage shows.
- **One backdrop logo remains, deliberately.** The Kjersti Flaa junket shot at
  ~3:40 has a "We're the Millers" step-and-repeat behind her. That is the
  physical set, not a broadcaster's bug, so no crop removes it without losing
  her — and a film backdrop under a block about her film career is honest.
- **Two of the six bites are voiceover, not sync** (`NOTPAIN`, `STRONG`, both
  from the Pvolve campaign). One of them, left alone, showed the Pvolve founder
  talking to camera while Aniston's voice played, which reads as though the
  other woman is speaking. Its picture is pointed at her own session instead.
  That swap is allowed only for voiceover bites — doing it to a sync bite would
  slide her lips off her voice.
