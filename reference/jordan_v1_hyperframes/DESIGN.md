# V4 design direction

Palette: red `#B22B1A`, black `#0D0D0D`, warm white `#F4F2EF`, gray `#A9A9A9`, charcoal `#252525`.

Typography: Anton for display; Archivo Black for labels; Archivo for support copy; Georgia only inside recreated editorial/source pages.

Picture language:

- High-resolution footage can run full-bleed; low-resolution archive is presented at native scale inside a film-study frame.
- Source scenes use real article captures or authentic archival imagery, paired with one editorial note and one accurately chosen red marker sweep.
- Chapter openers use a full-bleed photograph or video with a sliding magazine page, never masked letterforms.
- Feature spreads are asymmetric and image-led, with folios, source rails, wide margins, and a single dominant headline.
- Interview footage is framed like primary-source media and is never covered by unrelated B-roll.
- Chapter labels are small navigation elements, not full-screen cards.

Motion is deterministic and seek-safe. Page slides, clipped image reveals, drawn rules, waterfall headline arrivals, and subtle camera pushes are used selectively. Argument-level scenes hold long enough to read. There are no decorative exit animations.
